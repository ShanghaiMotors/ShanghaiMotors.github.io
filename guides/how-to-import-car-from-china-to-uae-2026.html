1. Why UAE Buyers Are Ordering Directly from China

In Dubai and Abu Dhabi, Chinese electric and hybrid vehicles have become some of the most sought-after cars on the road. Leading brands like Zeekr, Li Auto, Xiaomi, ROX, and Avatr offer luxury appointments, high-capacity liquid-cooled battery thermal systems engineered to easily withstand 50°C+ Gulf summer temperatures, and dual-motor AWD performance that rivals traditional European luxury marques.

However, local grey-market dealerships in the UAE often charge hefty markups of AED 40,000 to AED 100,000 over factory price. By purchasing directly from OEM factory allocations in China under official foreign trade contracts, UAE private buyers and commercial fleet operators cut out middlemen, securing factory-fresh vehicles with zero previous registration.

3. The 100% Original Document Package Sent to You

To guarantee that customs clearance at Jebel Ali Port is smooth, Shanghai Motors International prepares and couriers the complete original documentation package:

1. Original Bill of Lading (B/L) Official ocean transport title issued by the maritime shipping line (COSCO, Evergreen, or Maersk).
2. Attested Commercial Invoice Stating the vehicle's exact purchase price, VIN (Chassis number), engine code, and factory specifications.
3. Certificate of Origin (COO) Official certification confirming manufacture in China, verified by CCPIT.
4. Chinese Export Customs Release Legal deregistration certificate confirming clean title with zero prior road use.

4. What Happens When Your Car Arrives at Jebel Ali Port

Once the container vessel arrives at Jebel Ali Port, the local import process is straightforward. Most buyers simply hand the document pack to a licensed local port clearing agent (costing approximately AED 400 to AED 800), or handle it through the Dubai Trade portal:

  1. Delivery Order (DO): Present the original Bill of Lading to the shipping agent at Jebel Ali to obtain your cargo delivery order.
  2. Dubai Customs Declaration: Submit the commercial invoice and Certificate of Origin through Dubai Customs / Dubai Trade portal.
  3. Customs Duty & VAT: Settle the standard 5% customs duty and 5% VAT calculated on the CIF invoice value.
  4. Vehicle Clearance Certificate (VCC): Dubai Customs inspects the chassis number and issues the electronic VCC.
  5. RTA / Tasjeel Inspection & Plates: Complete the standard vehicle technical test at Tasjeel or Shamil, insure the vehicle, and receive your official UAE number plates.

7. Frequently Asked Questions by UAE Buyers

How much does ocean shipping cost from China to Jebel Ali?

Ocean container or Ro-Ro freight from Shanghai or Ningbo to Jebel Ali typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,000 (AED 4,400 to AED 7,350) per vehicle. Marine insurance covering 100% full commercial value is included in every CIF quotation.

What is the customs duty rate for importing cars into the UAE?

The UAE applies a 5% customs duty on the CIF value of the vehicle, plus 5% standard Value Added Tax (VAT) applied to the duty-inclusive amount (effective 10.25% combined tax rate).

Do Chinese EVs work reliably in UAE summer temperatures?

Yes. Contemporary models from Zeekr, Li Auto, and Xiaomi utilize advanced liquid battery cooling systems and heat pumps engineered and tested to maintain optimal thermal operating temperatures even in ambient desert heat exceeding 50°C.

Can I pay in UAE Dirhams (AED) or US Dollars?

Yes. Payments are executed under official commercial sales contracts via international bank wire transfer (SWIFT) in USD, AED, EUR, or CNY with transparent milestone billing.
